wolfCrypt's SP (single-precision) big-integer backend under wolfSSL's cryptofuzz-sp-math fuzz target (ASan, 32-bit build): computing the least common multiple of two large integers first allocates a small heap buffer sized for the digit count of one of the intermediate values, then feeds that value into wolfCrypt's big-integer division routine as part of the LCM computation; the division routine's internal digit-copy step ends up needing more machine words than the LCM routine actually allocated for that buffer, so the copy writes a few words (12 bytes) past the end of the undersized heap allocation, producing a heap-buffer-overflow write right at the block's boundary.
