Capstone's WASM disassembly printer (ASan build) repeatedly appends chunks of text into a single shared, fixed-size stack formatting buffer while printing one instruction's mnemonic/operands. Root cause: the helper responsible for each append computes "remaining writable bytes" as an unsigned subtraction of the already-written offset from the buffer's total capacity; once accumulated appends push that offset at or past the buffer's capacity, the subtraction underflows into a huge bogus remaining-capacity value, and the underlying formatted-write call then keeps writing up to that fake limit, running past the end of the stack buffer and causing a stack-buffer-overflow write.
